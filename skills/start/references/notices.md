## WELCOME

**Welcome to VA Claims Assistant.**

This free tool helps you build your VA disability claim, one step at a time.
Most steps are just buttons. You can stop anytime and pick up where you left off.

**What we'll do together:**

1. Answer a few quick questions about your service.
2. Point the app to a folder with your medical records.
3. Review a list of possible claims the app finds in your records.
4. Answer simple questions about how each condition affects your life.
5. Get a ready-to-file packet: a statement and the key evidence pages for each claim.

---

## WHAT THIS APP IS AND ISN'T

- It **is** a helper that organizes your records and drafts your paperwork.
- It **is not** a lawyer, a doctor, or the VA. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by
  the Department of Veterans Affairs. It cannot promise a rating or a result.
- It uses AI, and AI can make mistakes. Check every fact, date and quote against your
  records before you file. The app checks its own quotes, but you have the final say.
- You stay in charge. You review and approve everything before anything is filed.
- Everything it writes must be true and in your own words. It will never make up
  symptoms, events, or medical opinions.
- Free help from a trained, VA-accredited representative is always available:
  va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative

---

## YOUR PRIVACY

- Your records stay in **your** folder on **your** computer. The app never moves,
  renames, or changes your original files.
- Everything the app creates goes into a new folder called **VA-Claim-Output**
  inside your folder.
- While you use the app, Claude reads your records to find possible claims and
  draft your paperwork. That's how it helps. The pages it reads are processed in your
  own private Claude session (on your computer, or in a private workspace for your
  session), under the privacy terms of the Claude app you use.
- Nothing from your records is saved inside this app, and nothing is sent to the
  app's author. If you share the app with another Veteran, none of your information
  goes with it.
- Don't put your records or your claim folder in a shared project or a public place.

## FREE-USE NOTICE

> **⚠ THIS TOOL IS FREE. NO ONE SHOULD CHARGE YOU TO USE IT.**
>
> VA Claims Assistant is free for every Veteran. If anyone asks you to pay to use
> it, or charges you a fee to prepare your first (initial) VA claim, stop.
> According to VA, it is unlawful for anyone to charge a fee for preparing an
> initial claim.
>
> **Report it:** VSAFE.gov or call **1-833-38V-SAFE (1-833-388-7233)**.
>
> *Note: This app is always free. The program or service you use to run it
> (for example, an AI app subscription) may have its own cost. That cost goes to
> that company, not to this app, and it is not a fee for preparing your claim.*

## TAKE A BREAK

Some parts of a claim mean thinking about hard memories. You can skip any
question, take a break, or come back later — your progress is saved.
If you are in crisis, call or text **988, then press 1** (Veterans Crisis Line),
or text **838255**.

# How a Condition Gets Service-Connected (reference for later steps)
<!-- KILLROYWASHERE -->

Internal guide for Claude. Every section cited below is in `cfr/part-3/`. Always
open the cited file to confirm wording before relying on it. Tell the Veteran in
plain words, not legal terms.

## The three elements (every direct claim needs all three)

1. **Current diagnosis.** A disability diagnosed now (or at any time during the
   claim period).
2. **In-service event, injury, illness, or exposure.** Something that happened
   during service, shown in records or by credible lay statements (38 CFR 3.303(a);
   for combat Veterans, lay evidence of in-service events gets special weight under
   38 U.S.C. 1154(b)).
3. **Nexus.** A link between 1 and 2, usually a medical opinion ("at least as
   likely as not"). VA gives the benefit of the doubt when the evidence is evenly
   balanced (38 CFR 3.102).

Presumptive claims replace element 3 (and sometimes element 2) with a legal
presumption. Secondary claims replace element 2 with an existing service-connected
condition.

When evaluating a possible claim, mark each element **Found** (cite the
document and page), **Partial**, or **Missing**, and say what would fill the gap.

## Claim types

| Type | What it means | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** | Started in service, or caused by something in service | 3.303, 3.304 |
| **Chronic disease (1-year presumption)** | A listed chronic disease (e.g., arthritis, hypertension, diabetes, organic neurological disease, psychoses) showing up to at least 10% within 1 year after service | 3.307(a)(3), 3.309(a); continuity of symptoms for listed chronic diseases: 3.303(b) |
| **Aggravation of a pre-existing condition** | It existed before service and service made it worse | 3.304(b) (sound condition at entry), 3.306 |
| **Secondary** | Caused by a condition that is already service-connected | 3.310(a) |
| **Secondary aggravation** | A non-service condition made worse by a service-connected one; needs a baseline of severity | 3.310(b) |
| **TBI-related** | After service-connected TBI: Parkinsonism, seizures, certain dementias, depression, and pituitary-related conditions within set time frames | 3.310(d) |
| **Amputation → cardiovascular** | Heart disease after certain service-connected leg amputations | 3.310(c) |
| **Herbicide (Agent Orange)** | Exposure in listed places and times, plus a listed disease | 3.307(a)(6), 3.309(e) + `pact-act-supplement.md` |
| **Camp Lejeune water** | 30+ days at Camp Lejeune between 8/1/1953 and 12/31/1987, plus a listed disease | 3.307(a)(7), 3.309(f) |
| **Burn pits / particulate matter** | Qualifying Gulf War or post-9/11 location, plus a listed disease | 3.320, 3.320a, 3.320b + `pact-act-supplement.md` |
| **Gulf War undiagnosed / multisymptom illness** | Service in Southwest Asia, plus chronic fatigue syndrome, fibromyalgia, functional GI disorders (e.g., IBS), or undiagnosed illness for 6+ months | 3.317 (watch the date in (a)(1)(i)) |
| **Gulf War infectious diseases** | Listed infectious diseases (e.g., malaria, Q fever, West Nile) | 3.317(c) |
| **Radiation** | Radiation-exposed Veteran plus a listed disease | 3.309(d), 3.311 + `pact-act-supplement.md` |
| **Former POW** | Listed diseases at 10%+ any time after release | 3.309(c) |
| **Tropical diseases** | Listed tropical diseases within set time limits | 3.309(b) |
| **Mental health / PTSD** | PTSD stressor rules (combat, fear of hostile activity, personal assault/MST evidence alternatives) | 3.304(f) |

## Common secondary pairs (check when a service-connected condition exists)

These are common medical associations to **screen for**, not rules. Each still needs
a medical nexus opinion.

- Knee, hip, or ankle condition → opposite joint, back
- Back condition → radiculopathy (sciatica) of the legs
- PTSD or depression → sleep apnea (aggravation), hypertension, GERD/IBS, erectile dysfunction (often medication-related)
- Diabetes → peripheral neuropathy, erectile dysfunction, kidney disease, eye conditions
- Medication side effects (e.g., NSAIDs → GERD; SSRIs → sexual dysfunction)
- Tinnitus or hearing loss → anxiety or depression (if documented)
- TBI → headaches/migraines, see also 3.310(d)

## Guardrails

- Never tell the Veteran a claim **will** be granted. Use "possible claim" and
  "strength: strong / moderate / needs more evidence".
- Never invent or imply a medical opinion. If a nexus is missing, say a doctor's
  opinion would help, and offer the nexus-letter template later.
- For mental health and MST, use gentle language and make event details optional.

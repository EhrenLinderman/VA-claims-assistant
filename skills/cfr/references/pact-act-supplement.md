# PACT Act Supplement (statutory updates not yet reflected in the CFR text)
<!-- JOSHUAONENINE -->

Source: VA.gov, "The PACT Act and your VA benefits" and "Gulf War Veterans'
illnesses" pages. Checked 2026-10-05. The PACT Act (2022) set these by law
(38 U.S.C. 1116, 1119, 1120). Some aren't yet written into 38 CFR Part 3, but VA
applies them. When the CFR and this list differ, **include the condition as a
possible presumptive claim** and cite "PACT Act / VA.gov."

## Burn pits and other toxic exposures

**Qualifying locations: Gulf War era (on or after Aug 2, 1990)**
Bahrain, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, United Arab Emirates,
and the airspace above them.

**Qualifying locations: post-9/11 (on or after Sept 11, 2001)**
Afghanistan, Djibouti, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Uzbekistan, Yemen, and the
airspace above them.

**Presumptive cancers:** brain cancer; gastrointestinal cancer of any type;
glioblastoma; head cancer of any type; kidney cancer; lymphoma of any type;
melanoma; neck cancer of any type; pancreatic cancer; reproductive cancer of any
type; respiratory cancer of any type.

**Presumptive illnesses:** asthma diagnosed after service; chronic bronchitis;
COPD; chronic rhinitis; chronic sinusitis; constrictive or obliterative
bronchiolitis; emphysema; granulomatous disease; interstitial lung disease;
pleuritis; pulmonary fibrosis; sarcoidosis.

## Agent Orange

**Conditions added by the PACT Act:** high blood pressure (hypertension); MGUS
(monoclonal gammopathy of undetermined significance).
*Note: 3.309(e) Note 2 excludes hypertension from "ischemic heart disease." That
does not conflict with this list, because hypertension is now its own presumptive
condition by law.*

**Locations added by the PACT Act** (in addition to Vietnam 1/9/1962–5/7/1975,
the Korean DMZ 4/1/1968–8/31/1971, and C-123 aircraft in 3.307(a)(6)):
- Any U.S. or Royal Thai military base in Thailand: 1/9/1962 – 6/30/1976
- Laos: 12/1/1965 – 9/30/1969
- Cambodia (Mimot or Krek, Kampong Cham Province): 4/16/1969 – 4/30/1969
- Guam or American Samoa, or their territorial waters: 1/9/1962 – 7/31/1980
- Johnston Atoll, or a ship that called there: 1/1/1972 – 9/30/1977

## Radiation: response efforts added by the PACT Act

- Enewetak Atoll cleanup: 1/1/1977 – 12/31/1980
- Palomares, Spain B-52 cleanup: 1/17/1966 – 3/31/1967
- Thule AFB, Greenland B-52 fire response: 1/21/1968 – 9/25/1968

## Gulf War illness (3.317)

- **Locations (on or after 8/2/1990):** Southwest Asia theater, including Iraq,
  Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Afghanistan, and the waters of the Arabian Sea, Gulf of
  Aden, Gulf of Oman, Persian Gulf, and Red Sea (see 3.317 for the full list).
- **Requirement:** ill for at least 6 months.
- **Infectious diseases:** brucellosis, Campylobacter jejuni, Q fever, malaria,
  nontyphoid salmonella, shigella, and West Nile virus, if diagnosed within 1 year
  of separation; tuberculosis and visceral leishmaniasis at any time.
- **⚠ Date to watch:** the 38 CFR 3.317(a)(1)(i) text (as of 2026-10-01) requires
  undiagnosed/multisymptom illnesses to appear by **December 31, 2026**. VA has
  extended this date before. Check VA.gov before telling a Veteran it has passed.
